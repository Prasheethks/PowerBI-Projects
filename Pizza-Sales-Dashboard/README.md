# Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze pizza sales performance and identify business trends.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization

## Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify daily and monthly sales trends.
- Evaluate sales by pizza category and size.
- Identify best-selling and least-selling pizzas.
- Understand customer ordering patterns.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Pizzas Sold
- Average Order Value

## Key Insights
### 1. Overall Sales Performance
- Generated approximately 817.86K in total revenue during 2015.
- Recorded approximately 21K orders and sold around 50K pizzas.
- Average order value was 38.31.
- Average pizzas per order was 2.32, indicating that customers typically purchased more than one pizza per order.
### 2. Best-Selling Pizza Performance
- The Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue at approximately 43K.
- The Classic Deluxe Pizza recorded the highest quantity sold at approximately 2.5K.
- The Classic Deluxe Pizza also received the highest number of orders, at approximately 2.3K.
### 3. Lowest-Selling Pizza Performance
- The Brie Carre Pizza generated the lowest revenue at approximately 12K.
- It also recorded the lowest quantity sold, at approximately 490.
- It received the fewest orders, at approximately 480.
### 4. Ordering Trends
- Friday recorded the highest daily order volume at approximately 3.5K orders.
- Thursday followed with approximately 3.2K orders.
- July recorded the highest monthly order volume at approximately 1,935 orders.
- October recorded the lowest monthly order volume at approximately 1,646 orders.
### 5. Category and Size Preferences
- The Classic category contributed the highest share of sales at approximately 26.91%.
- The Large pizza size accounted for the highest sales share at approximately 45.89%.
- The Veggie category contributed approximately 23.68% of sales.
### 6. Recommendations
Based on these findings, the business could consider:
- Promote popular products: Use the popularity of Classic Deluxe and Thai Chicken pizzas in promotional campaigns.
- Focus on peak days: Plan inventory and staffing to handle higher order volumes on Fridays.
- Investigate weaker products: Review the pricing, customer feedback and demand for Brie Carre Pizza.
- Optimize product availability: Ensure sufficient stock of Large-sized pizzas, which contribute the largest share of sales.
- Investigate seasonal trends: Explore why July has the highest monthly order volume and October the lowest.
